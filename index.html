Free framework you take away

Jo bika, wohi order

Order only what sold. The medical-store register, on your phone.

  • EkWhen an item leaves the counter, tap it once on your phone. Two seconds.
  • DoAt order time the list is already made: what sold, and how much.
  • TeenSet minimum stock and MOQ, and the order quantity builds itself.

One habit fixes three of the five mistakes. No computer, no training, no monthly fees. Anyone who runs WhatsApp can run it, and your data stays on your own phone.

The author

I did not inherit this business.

Er. Deepanshu Agarwal · Manufacturing · Wholesale · Retail

No family factory waiting to be taken over. No established brand to build on. No ready list of customers. What I had instead was a broken factory and a reason to fix it.

I am an engineer by education and a plywood business strategist by choice. I have run this trade from all three sides of the counter, which is why I write about the business and not about sheets.

  • 2012A small unit in Bareilly, carrying serious debt, on machinery the market had already outgrown.
  • Six monthsRepairing and replacing almost every machine on the floor, before a single saleable sheet.
  • Feb 2013The first sheet came off the rebuilt line. Not the end of the work, the beginning of it.
  • Nov 2015My brother and I opened our own retail counter and a wholesale operation, to learn the business from the inside.

What I stand for

Quality never fails

My father's rule, and the one I have tested at every stage since. When the product is good and the intention is right, you are always on the right track.

 

Goodwill is the real asset

A retailer's most valuable asset is not his stock or his location. It is the trust he has built, one guarantee at a time. It should be protected, not spent.
